The one thing to weigh before choosing

D2 is the faithful reproduction, and it is the one that most directly contradicts what this project is for. It says “10 Homes For Sale”, shows a map with pins, and then — twenty lines lower — says “nothing in the corpus matches”. A reader meets both in one scroll.

  • Panda holds 10 facts. It has no listings, no photos and no geocoded inventory.
  • A photo grid promises property that the system cannot evidence.
  • The amber card only reads as honest if the surrounding design stops implying inventory.
  • D1 and D3 keep the reference’s craft while letting every number remain traceable.
