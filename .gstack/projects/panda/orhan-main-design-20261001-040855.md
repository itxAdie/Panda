## Problem Statement

Property search in Lahore runs on listings plus broker tribal knowledge. A buyer can
find 8,000 listings for DHA Phase 6 and still not know whether a 10-marla there will
have possession, whether the block's litigation cleared, what a file actually
transfers for versus what it asks for, or what the total cost lands at after fees
and taxes. That knowledge exists — but as dated prose scattered across dozens of
competing agency blogs, each pumping its own inventory.

The product is a **risk-and-possession knowledge layer** for Lahore housing schemes,
exposed through a conversational interface (English + Roman Urdu). Chat is the
interface, not the product. The corpus is the product.

## What Makes This Cool

Ask "is DHA Phase 6 Block D a safe buy in 2026" and get an answer that:

- Names the block and states what is actually known about it
- **Cites the source and the date** for every claim
- **Says when it doesn't know** rather than inventing a number
- Will later tell you that three agencies disagree on price and which one is
  pumping its own listings

The demo moment: ask a question no portal can answer, get a sourced, dated,
honest answer, then ask a follow-up that the engine visibly narrows on.

Conventional wisdom in this space: *harvest structured listing data, because it's
clean and it already exists.*

**EUREKA:** The low-information structured data is what everyone already has. The
high-information layer is the dated prose nobody has structured. And it is
adversarially unreliable in a *systematic, exploitable* way — every agency blog is
pumping its own inventory, so bias is directional and detectable, not random noise.
That is a ranking signal, not a cleanup problem.

## Constraints

- Side project, no deadline, no money
- Greenfield — empty directory, no existing code to reuse
- Buyer-side only (rent was considered and rejected)
- Lahore only. Karachi/Islamabad expansion is out of scope until the pipeline is proven
- Legal risk: nothing here is legal advice. Every output must carry a disclaimer
  pointing to a lawyer and the relevant authority (DHA/LDA)

## Premises

Agreed in session:

1. Conversational property search already ships in Pakistan. PropertyWalay and
   PropertyAI.pk both do it; the latter has 15 years of field expertise behind it.
2. Block-level price intelligence for Lahore is already built. GharPulse scrapes
   Zameen across 8,000+ listings and 21 societies, and Zameen has its own price index.
3. The unharvested layer is the risk/possession knowledge: dated rate tables,
   affidavit-vs-allocation distinctions, balloting schedules, litigation status,
   NDC/FRC transfer mechanics, possession timelines, fee changes.
4. Therefore: the risk-and-possession layer is the product. Chat and price are
   supporting features.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Source Registry + Batch Extract — CHOSEN

Pick the 8-10 Lahore agency/blog sources that publish dated rate and status tables.
One script fetches, LLM extracts typed facts into SQLite as
`(society, block, fact_type, value, as_of, source_url)`. Chat queries the table and
renders `as_of` dates inline.

Effort: S (a weekend). Risk: Low.
Pros: working end-to-end fast; provenance is free; extraction errors are visible by
inspecting the source URL. Cons: goes stale when re-runs stop; no contradiction
detection; confidence is a constant rather than computed.
Reuses: nothing — greenfield.

### Approach B: Contradiction Engine — v2, NOT v1

Same extraction, but facts become **claims**. On write, compare against existing
claims: agreement raises confidence, systematic disagreement marks the attribute
contested, and each source's known inventory bias is down-weighted. Answers cite
confidence and name the disagreement.

Effort: L. Risk: Med. Chosen as v2 because Approach A's schema (fact carries source
and date) absorbs the claim model without re-ingestion.

### Approach C: Decay Engine — rejected for now

Store how confident we are each fact is true *today*. Half-lives per fact type:
balloting dates weeks, possession timelines months, fees a quarter, NDC procedure
rules years. Core output is an admission, not a fact.

Rejected because half-life values are guesses until a user-correction loop exists to
calibrate them. Revisit after B, where per-source accuracy data makes decay estimable.

### Also considered and dropped

- **Broker-bypass deal engine** — sharpest business, but overlaps GharPulse's ground.
- **Negotiation copilot** — later-stage funnel; the wedge is discovery, not closing.
- **Interviewing brokers/residents** — highest truth, doesn't scale past one area.

## Recommended Approach

**Approach A now, Approach B as v2.**

A weekend pipeline producing real extracted facts is the only thing that makes the
rest argueable. Ship provenance first — a fact you cannot trace to a dated source is
worthless in this market, and it is the one thing incumbents visibly do not do.

Concretely, the v1 corpus targets these fact types, in priority order:

| fact_type | Example | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `rate` | 10 marla allocation, DHA Ph 10 = PKR 43.75L | sanity check against listings |
| `balloting_status` | Ph 10 Prism, H Block litigation cleared Jul 2025 | near-term catalyst, decays fastest |
| `possession_estimate` | Ph 10 blocks A/B/C, 3–5 years | the question buyers actually ask |
| `file_type_terms` | affidavit vs allocation, 1.5% vs 4.5% transfer tax | drives total cost |
| `fee_schedule` | 1 kanal membership Rs 150k, up from 75k | dated, verifiable, changes |
| `transfer_process` | NDC mandatory, 7–15 working days after NDC | stable, high value |
| `litigation_status` | Phase 9 Prism H Block cleared | binary, high signal |
| `tax_regime` | buyer advance tax 236K 3%→1.5%; non-filer cap Rs 50M | changes with budget |

Normalize to `City > Society > Phase > Block` before storing anything. `DHA Ph 6`,
`DHA Phase VI`, `Defence Phase 6`, and `DHA Lahore Phase 6 Block D` are one entity.
Anything that cannot be mapped confidently is dropped, never guessed.

## The Assignment

**Do not extract anything yet.** No scraper, no LLM extraction prompt, no ingestion
run. Three artifacts first, in this order:

1. **Enumerate 8–10 Lahore sources** publishing dated property rates and/or status
   information. Record what each publishes, its cadence, and its last update date.
   Lahore schemes only: DHA, Bahria Town, LDA City, Gulberg, Askari.
2. **Hand-author the normalization map**: `society → phase → block`, canonicalized,
   with every observed naming variant included.
3. **Preserve the original source string alongside every canonical entity.** The
   alias table is the audit trail proving what was merged and from what. Never
   discard the raw string.

Rules, non-negotiable:

- **Drop anything that cannot be mapped confidently. Never guess.**
- **The terms-of-service question is a hard gate.** Resolve it before any source is
  harvested — not during, not after. See Open Question 1.
- Only after all three artifacts exist: run `/plan-eng-review` to stress-test entity
  resolution. Extraction code comes after that review.

## Why Entity Resolution Is the Foundation

The map is the artifact that, if wrong, makes everything downstream **internally
consistent and still wrong**. This failure mode is invisible from inside the system:
extraction looks clean, retrieval returns results, answers cite real URLs and real
dates — and every fact is confidently attached to the wrong block. Nothing errors,
nothing looks broken, and the corpus is about a place that does not exist.

GharPulse makes the same call — *"an inaccurate data point is worse than a missing
one"* — and they have the benefit of having already shipped. That is why the map
precedes extraction rather than following it.

## Open Questions

1. **Terms of service — HARD GATE.** Scraping agency blogs is legally and socially
   delicate, and this blocks harvesting. Unresolved: does the pipeline rate-limit and
   identify itself, read as a normal client, or seek permission per source? Must be
   settled before any source is touched.
2. **Source count vs depth.** 8 sources across all of Lahore is shallow. 3 sources
   across DHA alone is deep. Which v1?
3. **Roman Urdu coverage.** Every source found is English. Where does Roman Urdu
   demand — queries, or answers — actually come from? Facebook groups and YouTube
   transcripts are the likely source and neither has been assessed.
4. **Legal disclaimer UX.** How prominently does "this is not legal advice" appear
   before it kills the conversational feel?
5. **Feedback loop.** The signal for B's per-source accuracy needs user corrections.
   Where do those come from in v1?
6. **Comparison against GharPulse.** Their price bands are the obvious competitor
   for the `rate` fact type. Build anyway, or start at balloting/possession/litigation
   and leave price to them?

## Success Criteria

Pre-extraction gates (must pass before any scraper is written):

- 8–10 Lahore sources enumerated, each with publication cadence and last update date
- Normalization map covers every society/phase/block observed across those sources
- Zero unmapped entity strings; anything ambiguous is dropped and logged as dropped
- Every canonical entity retains its full set of observed source-string aliases
- Terms-of-service decision recorded before any source is fetched

Post-extraction (later, only after /plan-eng-review clears):

- Pipeline extracts ≥200 facts covering ≥5 Lahore societies, every one carrying a
  working source URL and an `as_of` date
- Conversational layer answers "is X safe to buy" with sourced, dated claims and
  names the areas it has no data for instead of guessing
- Rate facts from 3 sources on the same block manually spot-checked; extraction
  accuracy ≥90%

## Distribution Plan

Self-hosted web app, single container, no auth in v1. Deployed on a single VPS or
fly.io. No package to distribute — the deliverable is the web service. CI is manual;
there is no release artifact beyond a container build.

## Next Steps

Ordered by dependency. Steps 1-4 are the assignment; step 5 is a review gate; nothing
downstream of step 5 may begin until it clears.

1. **Resolve the terms-of-service gate.** Decide how the pipeline identifies itself
   and whether per-source permission is required. Blocks everything below.
2. **Enumerate 8-10 Lahore sources.** Record what each publishes, cadence, last update.
   DHA, Bahria Town, LDA City, Gulberg, Askari.
3. **Hand-author the normalization map** — `society → phase → block`, canonicalized,
   every observed variant included, original source string preserved per entity.
   Highest-leverage artifact in the project.
4. **Dry-run the map against every source.** For each enumerated source, manually
   resolve its entity strings through the map. Log every miss. Ambiguous strings get
   dropped and recorded as dropped — never guessed.
5. **`/plan-eng-review` — entity resolution gate.** Stress-test the map design,
   alias handling, and drop-logging before any extraction code is written.
6. *After the review clears:* design the fact schema — `(canonical_entity, fact_type,
   value, as_of, source_url, raw_quote)`. `raw_quote` is non-negotiable; it is how
   extraction gets audited without re-reading the source.
7. *After the review clears:* single-source extraction end to end, one page, raw
   quotes included, before touching the other nine.
8. *After the review clears:* batch ingest the remaining sources.
9. *After the review clears:* chat interface over the table — must cite source and
   date for every claim, must say "I don't have data on that area" when it doesn't.
10. *Only then:* contradiction detection (Approach B).

Deliberately skipped in v1: voice input, price bands, mobile app, user accounts,
deal negotiation, Karachi/Islamabad.
