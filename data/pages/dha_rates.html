Allocation File vs Affidavit File – What Is the Difference?

One of the most common questions in the DHA property market is the difference between an allocation file and an affidavit file.

Generally, buyers should understand that these are different categories of property documentation and may have different market values and transaction procedures.

A buyer should never rely solely on the terminology used in an advertisement. Before purchasing, verify the original documentation and current status through the relevant DHA process.

Before buying any DHA file, check:

  1. Original file/documentation
  2. Seller ownership
  3. CNIC and ownership verification
  4. File number
  5. Allocation status
  6. Ballot status
  7. Payment history
  8. Outstanding dues
  9. Transferability
  10. Applicable taxes and charges
  11. DHA record verification
  12. Any restrictions, objections or legal issues

How to Buy DHA Lahore Files Safely

Buying a DHA file is a significant investment. Whether you are interested in DHA Phase 10 files, Phase 13 files, Phase 9 Prism files, Phase 7 files or Phase 9 Town files, due diligence is essential.

Step 1 – Select the DHA Phase

Determine whether you want to invest in:

  • DHA Phase 7
  • DHA Phase 9 Prism
  • DHA Phase 9 Town
  • DHA Phase 10 / Phase X
  • DHA Phase 13
  • Other DHA Lahore phases

Step 2 – Select File Size

Common sizes include:

  • 5 Marla
  • 8 Marla
  • 10 Marla
  • 20 Marla / 1 Kanal
  • 2 Kanal
  • Commercial files

Step 3 – Confirm File Category

Ask whether the file is:

  • Allocation
  • Affidavit
  • Commercial
  • Barcode
  • Balloted
  • Non-balloted
  • Any other applicable category

Step 4 – Verify the Seller

Make sure the person selling the file has legitimate ownership and authority to sell it.

Step 5 – Verify the File

Do not make payment merely because a file is advertised at an attractive price. Verify its record, status and transferability.

Step 6 – Compare Market Rates

Compare rates from multiple sources and understand whether the quoted price is a demand price, offer price or actual transaction price.

Step 7 – Complete the Transfer Properly

Use the applicable DHA transfer procedure and retain all transaction documents and receipts.

DHA Lahore Files as an Investment

DHA Lahore files are frequently searched by investors because they can provide an alternative way to participate in the DHA property market without immediately purchasing a developed house or constructed property.

However, file investment is not risk-free.

Potential investors should consider:

Market Risk: File prices can rise or fall.

Liquidity Risk: A file may not always sell immediately at the desired price.

Documentation Risk: Incorrect or incomplete documentation can create serious problems.

Development Risk: Future development timelines can affect market prices.

Policy Risk: DHA rules, government regulations, taxes and transfer procedures can change.

Demand Risk: Different phases and file sizes can experience different levels of demand.

Therefore, investors should conduct proper verification before making a decision.
