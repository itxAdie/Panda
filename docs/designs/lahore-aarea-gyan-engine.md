## Problem Statement

Property search in Lahore runs on listings plus broker tribal knowledge. A buyer can
find 8,000 listings for DHA Phase 6 and still not know whether a 10-marla there will
have possession, whether the block's litigation cleared, what a file actually
transfers for versus what it asks for, or what the total cost lands at after fees
and taxes. That knowledge exists — but as dated prose scattered across dozens of
competing agency blogs, each pumping its own inventory.

The product is a **risk-and-possession knowledge layer** for Lahore housing schemes,
exposed through a conversational interface (English + Roman Urdu). Chat is the
interface, not the product. The corpus is the product.

## What Makes This Cool

Ask "is DHA Phase 6 Block D a safe buy in 2026" and get an answer that:

- Names the block and states what is actually known about it
- **Cites the source and the date** for every claim
- **Says when it doesn't know** rather than inventing a number
- Will later tell you that three agencies disagree on price and which one is
  pumping its own listings

The demo moment: ask a question no portal can answer, get a sourced, dated,
honest answer, then ask a follow-up that the engine visibly narrows on.

Conventional wisdom in this space: *harvest structured listing data, because it's
clean and it already exists.*

**EUREKA:** The low-information structured data is what everyone already has. The
high-information layer is the dated prose nobody has structured. And it is
adversarially unreliable in a *systematic, exploitable* way — every agency blog is
pumping its own inventory, so bias is directional and detectable, not random noise.
That is a ranking signal, not a cleanup problem.

## Constraints

- Side project, no deadline, no money
- Greenfield — empty directory, no existing code to reuse
- Buyer-side only (rent was considered and rejected)
- Lahore only. Karachi/Islamabad expansion is out of scope until the pipeline is proven
- Legal risk: nothing here is legal advice. Every output must carry a disclaimer
  pointing to a lawyer and the relevant authority (DHA/LDA)

## Premises

Agreed in session:

1. Conversational property search already ships in Pakistan. PropertyWalay and
   PropertyAI.pk both do it; the latter has 15 years of field expertise behind it.
2. Block-level price intelligence for Lahore is already built. GharPulse scrapes
   Zameen across 8,000+ listings and 21 societies, and Zameen has its own price index.
3. The unharvested layer is the risk/possession knowledge: dated rate tables,
   affidavit-vs-allocation distinctions, balloting schedules, litigation status,
   NDC/FRC transfer mechanics, possession timelines, fee changes.
4. Therefore: the risk-and-possession layer is the product. Chat and price are
   supporting features.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Source Registry + Batch Extract — CHOSEN

Pick the 8-10 Lahore agency/blog sources that publish dated rate and status tables.
One script fetches, LLM extracts typed facts into SQLite as
`(society, block, fact_type, value, as_of, source_url)`. Chat queries the table and
renders `as_of` dates inline.

Effort: S (a weekend). Risk: Low.
Pros: working end-to-end fast; provenance is free; extraction errors are visible by
inspecting the source URL. Cons: goes stale when re-runs stop; no contradiction
detection; confidence is a constant rather than computed.
Reuses: nothing — greenfield.

### Approach B: Contradiction Engine — v2, NOT v1

Same extraction, but facts become **claims**. On write, compare against existing
claims: agreement raises confidence, systematic disagreement marks the attribute
contested, and each source's known inventory bias is down-weighted. Answers cite
confidence and name the disagreement.

Effort: L. Risk: Med. Chosen as v2 because Approach A's schema (fact carries source
and date) absorbs the claim model without re-ingestion.

### Approach C: Decay Engine — rejected for now

Store how confident we are each fact is true *today*. Half-lives per fact type:
balloting dates weeks, possession timelines months, fees a quarter, NDC procedure
rules years. Core output is an admission, not a fact.

Rejected because half-life values are guesses until a user-correction loop exists to
calibrate them. Revisit after B, where per-source accuracy data makes decay estimable.

### Also considered and dropped

- **Broker-bypass deal engine** — sharpest business, but overlaps GharPulse's ground.
- **Negotiation copilot** — later-stage funnel; the wedge is discovery, not closing.
- **Interviewing brokers/residents** — highest truth, doesn't scale past one area.

## Recommended Approach

**Approach A now, Approach B as v2.**

A weekend pipeline producing real extracted facts is the only thing that makes the
rest argueable. Ship provenance first — a fact you cannot trace to a dated source is
worthless in this market, and it is the one thing incumbents visibly do not do.

Concretely, the v1 corpus targets these fact types, in priority order:

| fact_type | Example | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `rate` | 10 marla allocation, DHA Ph 10 = PKR 43.75L | sanity check against listings |
| `balloting_status` | Ph 10 Prism, H Block litigation cleared Jul 2025 | near-term catalyst, decays fastest |
| `possession_estimate` | Ph 10 blocks A/B/C, 3–5 years | the question buyers actually ask |
| `file_type_terms` | affidavit vs allocation, 1.5% vs 4.5% transfer tax | drives total cost |
| `fee_schedule` | 1 kanal membership Rs 150k, up from 75k | dated, verifiable, changes |
| `transfer_process` | NDC mandatory, 7–15 working days after NDC | stable, high value |
| `litigation_status` | Phase 9 Prism H Block cleared | binary, high signal |
| `tax_regime` | buyer advance tax 236K 3%→1.5%; non-filer cap Rs 50M | changes with budget |

Normalize to `City > Society > Phase > Block` before storing anything. `DHA Ph 6`,
`DHA Phase VI`, `Defence Phase 6`, and `DHA Lahore Phase 6 Block D` are one entity.
Anything that cannot be mapped confidently is dropped, never guessed.

## Why Entity Resolution Is the Foundation

The map is the artifact that, if wrong, makes everything downstream **internally
consistent and still wrong**. This failure mode is invisible from inside the system:
extraction looks clean, retrieval returns results, answers cite real URLs and real
dates — and every fact is confidently attached to the wrong block. Nothing errors,
nothing looks broken, and the corpus is about a place that does not exist.

GharPulse makes the same call — *"an inaccurate data point is worse than a missing
one"* — and they have the benefit of having already shipped. That is why the map
precedes extraction rather than following it.

## Schema Changes For /plan-eng-review — EXPLICIT REVIEW INPUTS

The dry-run exposed a structural defect, not merely missing aliases. These six items
are the review agenda. `/plan-eng-review` must address each one by name.

### R1. Compositional name resolution
Parse society qualifiers and phase designators as **independent slots**, then
validate that the resulting entity actually exists under that society.

```
{ "", "DHA ", "DHA Lahore ", "Defence " } x { "Phase N", "Ph N", "Phase-N", "PhaseN" } x optional parenthetical
```

**Never accept a match because the text resembles a known name.** A resemblance is a
hypothesis, not a resolution. The cross-product is unbounded, so alias enumeration
cannot scale; only composition can. Aliases are retained for genuinely irregular
forms that do not decompose: `Phase X`, `Ex-DHA City`, `M-Extension`, `M-Extension`.

### R2. Society-specific hierarchies
Replace the global `level` enum with a per-society `place_type` declaration. DHA
partitions by block; Bahria by sector; LDA City by **both** block and sector.
Resolution must respect the declaring society's grammar rather than assume one tree.

### R3. Explicit sub-sector level
Add `sub_sector` as a first-class level. Two distinctions must survive:

- **DHA Rahbar Sector 4 is a sector *within* Phase 11.** The current map wrongly
  models `Rahbar Sec-4` as a phase-level alias, flattening a level. Facts about the
  sector and the phase as a whole would merge into one entity — sourced, dated, and
  wrong.
- **DHA Phase 9 Prism has lettered sectors** (L, R, B, F, G, H, N, Q) that the map
  omits entirely, in the phase where most 2026 activity sits.

Note the collision this creates: `DHA Phase 9 Prism 10` — that `10` is a **sector**,
while the `5` in `DHA Phase 9 Town 5` is a **size**. Identical surface form,
different dimensions.

### R4. Permanent observation records
`observation` becomes a permanent table, not a dry-run artifact. Every unmatched,
ambiguous, and rejected string is retained with source, occurrence count,
adjudication status, and the reason. **Drops are signal.** They show where source
terminology outruns the map, and they are the only defence against a silent
coverage regression.

### R5. Two sources still unverified
`maanestate.com` returned HTTP 403 and `asaspropertiespk.com` was unreachable from
this host. Neither has been content-tested. Their coverage is **assumed, not
verified**. Keep them marked unverified in the source registry. If access cannot be
obtained, either drop them from the corpus or carry the unverified flag into every
fact attributed to them — never let assumed coverage read as measured coverage.

### R6. Never force a match to improve coverage statistics — ADOPTED, REFRAMED
Coverage is measured **offline only**. The resolution function accepts only: the
observed string, its document context, and the entity map. No coverage metric is
readable from the resolution path, so none can be optimised.

The bare `Sector A` / `Sector B` drops are the proof: eight occurrences went
unresolved during the dry-run, and that was the correct outcome. A forced match would
have raised the resolution rate and silently corrupted the corpus — attaching Bahria
Sector A facts to Askari 10 Sector A, both real, both sourced, both wrong.

## Milestone 1 — Deterministic Foundation (FIRST CODE)

Build this before any LLM extraction. It is the load-bearing layer: if resolution is
wrong, no amount of extraction quality saves the corpus.

Pipeline: `raw source text -> entity normalization -> validated area facts -> provenance`

### Required contract

The resolution function accepts **only**: the observed string, its document context,
and the entity map. No coverage metric is readable from this path (R6, structurally
enforced).

**Precondition order — non-negotiable:**

1. Apply drops (cross-city, sector-letter, not-a-place)
2. Measure/CCA normalization via `measurement.csv` (category, never place)
3. Compositional parse — **society slot AND phase slot**
4. Existence validation against `entity.csv`
5. Alias lookup

Step 1 must precede canonical matching. If it does not, bare `DHA Phase N` resolves
to Lahore by canonical name and silently absorbs Islamabad data — demonstrated in
`rerun-results.md`, worth 7.6 points of false resolution.

### Django models (stack is Django end to end)

- `Society`, `Place` (`parent`, `canonical_name`, `evidence`, `evidence_status`)
- `Alias` (`alias_text`, `FK Place`, `evidence`, `evidence_url`, `confirmed`)
- `Drop` (`alias_text`, `reason`, `evidence_state`)
- `Observation` (`observed`, `source`, `document`, `occurrences`, `adjudication`,
  `resolved_to`, `reason`) — permanent, never pruned
- `Document` (`source`, `url`, `fetched_at`, `channel`) — `channel` distinguishes
  live page from AI hub (R5 residue)
- `Fact` (`place`, `fact_type`, `value`, `as_of`, `document`, `raw_quote`)
- `FactSourceStatus` (`source`, `verified`, `channel`)

Invariants:
- No alias or observation may reference a nonexistent `Place`
- No `confirmed=true` alias may have evidence contradicting its mapping (the Rahbar
  class of bug)
- `raw_quote` is mandatory on every fact — it is the audit path
- Drops applied before canonical matching — assert in tests, not comments

## Extraction Boundary — LLM Contract (DECIDED, not yet implemented)

**Rule: every LLM output must be reconstructible from its quote by a deterministic
function. If no deterministic check can verify it, it does not enter the knowledge
base.**

This follows from the project's failure mode. Entity resolution is safe because it
is deterministic and refuses to guess. Hand the same decision to an LLM and you get
the internally-consistent-but-wrong corpus back — fluent, confident, and carrying a
real citation.

### The LLM never names a place

The single most important line. The extractor is **handed a `Place` id**. Wrong entity
attachment is the one failure this project has spent its entire life preventing, and it
is the failure an LLM is most likely to reintroduce.

### Permitted output tiers

| Tier | Content | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Extractable** | rate, size, file type, possession/balloting status, fee schedule, `as_of` | value must appear **literally** in the quote |
| 2 — Derived | price bands, ranges, comparisons | **NOT PERMITTED in v1.** Rejected at the boundary |
| 3 — Refused | anything absent from the document | never |

Tier 1 only, decided 2026-10-02. Rationale: a derived value that cannot be recomputed
when its inputs are corrected is precisely the internally-consistent-but-wrong number
this project exists to prevent. Recomputation infrastructure must exist before
derivation is allowed.

### Deterministic validators — all must pass

| ID | Check | Rejects |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | `raw_quote` is a normalized substring of the document text | paraphrase, reconstruction, fabricated quotes |
| **V2** | every number in `value` appears in `raw_quote` | inferred or hallucinated rates |
| **V3** | `as_of` appears in the quote or document metadata | invented dates |
| **V4** | `Place` came from the resolver, never the model | wrong entity attachment |
| **V5** | the `CROSS_CITY` guard applies to fact text | Islamabad facts attaching to Lahore |
| **V6** | `CCA-*` routes to measurement, never a place fact | commercial categories becoming geography |
| **V7** | a fact can never set `BlockIdentity.verified` | LLM-driven promotion past the 2-source rule |

V4 and V7 are structural: a fact carrying an invented `Place` id has no referent and
cannot be constructed, and no code path from extraction reaches the promotion flag.

### Failure classes this kills

hallucinated rates · wrong entity attachment · invented dates · paraphrased quotes ·
affidavit/allocation conflation · cross-city contamination · CCA becoming a place ·
LLM-driven identity promotion

### Rejection handling

A rejected candidate is persisted as an `Observation` with `adjudication='dropped'`
and the validator id in `reason`. **Rejections are never retried into acceptance and
never silently discarded** — same policy as unresolved strings in Milestone 1.

Rejected volume is a first-class diagnostic. A validator that rejects a large share of
candidates is either mis-tuned or the LLM is being asked something the sources cannot
support; both are worth seeing.

### Explicitly out of scope for extraction

Chat interface · contradiction detection (Approach B) · price bands · any Tier-2
derivation · Karachi/Islamabad · voice.

## Milestone 7 — Fact qualifiers + LiteLLM: DONE ✅

**152 tests.** `Fact` now carries `(place, size, file_type, amount)`.

### Qualifier contract (V11)

A rate is only meaningful as `(place, size, file_type, amount)`. v1 stored a bare
number, so `133.00 Lacs` and `218 Lacs` were indistinguishable from a population of
equally valid, differently-qualified rates.

- **A qualifier the quote states MUST be supplied.** Omitting it is rejected — an
  unqualified rate is indistinguishable from other rates.
- **A qualifier the quote does not state must be left UNKNOWN.** Inferring it would be
  fabrication.
- `qualifier_status` is `complete | partial | unknown` — an explicit, queryable state,
  not an ambiguous NULL. A NULL column cannot be distinguished from a bug.
- `as_qualified_string()` never silently drops a qualifier: it renders
  `4.00 marla affidavit = 218 Lacs (complete)` or `... size unknown`.

Rule detail worth noting: the file-type requirement fires when the quote names **one**
file type *and* when it names **several**. An affidavit and an allocation at the same
size are different assets at different prices — that is the hardest disambiguation
case, so it is the one enforced.

`amount_pkr` normalises lakh/crore to PKR. This is a **unit conversion on a stated
number**, not a Tier-2 derived fact: V2 has already proved the figure appears verbatim,
and lakh→PKR is arithmetic on units, not interpretation.

### LIVE RUN — 4th real defect found: THE CONTRACT NEVER REACHED THE MODEL

**The hosted gateway DROPS litellm's `system=` parameter.** Probed directly:

| Delivery method | Result |
|---|---|
| `system=` parameter | **DROPPED** — told "reply with exactly BANANA", model said "Hello! How can I help you today?" |
| message with `role="system"` | honoured |
| instructions inside the user message | honoured |

`extract.py` passed the contract via `system=`. Through this gateway that parameter was
discarded, so **the model received the page text and no instructions whatsoever.**

The silent failure is the dangerous part: the model returned a well-formed markdown
summary of the rate table — reproducing the numbers almost verbatim, so it was clearly
*working* — but emitted zero candidates and **nothing errored**. `0 candidates` looked
identical to "the model found nothing in this page". An empty result meaning "the
instructions were lost" is the worst failure shape available.

Fixed minimally: gateway mode sends the contract as `{"role": "system", ...}`; SDK mode
keeps `system=`, which works there. Three tests cover both paths, including that the
contract text is present.

Also fixed: `run_live_page` wrote the candidates file as a bare list while `FileProposer`
expects `{source_slug: [rows]}`.

3. **`dha lahore` was a marker.** It appears on every DHA page, so it corroborated

### KNOWN GAP — not mechanically catchable

**V8 checks that a `fact_type` is in the allowed SET, not that it matches the
quote's content.** A balloting fact filed as `possession_status` passes every

## Invariant: absence of evidence is not evidence of absence

**The answer surface must distinguish two fundamentally different claims:**

| Claim | Requires | Must never be substituted by |
|---|---|---|
| "the corpus contains no supporting evidence" | a search over stored facts that found nothing | anything else |
| "no such property / fact exists in the market" | an exhaustive, authoritative source | **never** |

Panda is not an authoritative source. Every rate is an *asking* price reported by a
brokerage blog; every possession and balloting status is second-hand reporting of a DHA
notice. Nothing in this corpus can support a negative claim about the market. The
difference is not cosmetic — one is *"I have nothing on this"* and the other is
*"this does not exist"*, and a buyer acting on the second one loses money.

**Where this must hold:**

1. The answer layer must state the corpus's coverage when a query yields nothing, and
   name what it searched. No silent empty result.
2. The answer layer must never convert "no fact matched" into "no match exists".
3. Single-source facts must be visibly weaker than corroborated ones. 56 of 57 block
   identities rest on one publisher and must read that way.
4. `qualifier_status == unknown` must surface in the answer, not be smoothed over. A
   rate with an unknown size is not a complete rate.
5. A place with no facts is not an area with no property. Absence of a fact for
   `DHA Phase 10 Block Q` says nothing about that block's actual inventory.

**Already structurally supported:** the boundary refuses to guess, every fact carries a
`raw_quote` and `source`, and the promotion rule keeps single-source identities
flagged. What is NOT yet built is the layer that renders a zero-result answer, and that
layer is where this invariant will be easiest to break.

**Acceptance criterion for the eventual surface:** given a query with no supporting
evidence, the answer names the coverage that was searched and says plainly that the
corpus holds nothing on it. A test must assert the phrase "does not exist" (or any
equivalent negative market claim) is never produced by a zero-result path.
